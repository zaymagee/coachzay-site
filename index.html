THE STORY.

Mindful Hoops Academy was founded by Isaiah 'Zay' Magee, whose own basketball journey taught him that elite performance isn't just physical — it's mental. After earning 1st Team All-Region (5-7A) and MVP honors while playing through injury, Zay discovered a critical truth: young athletes need more than drills. They need guidance on mental resilience, body awareness, and life skills that extend beyond the court. That realization became a philosophy: Think. Train. Dominate.

WHAT TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.

What ages do you train?

Athletes from kindergarten through high school, grouped by age and skill level so every player is challenged at the right level.

Where do sessions run?

Sessions run at Mindful Hoops Academy locations across Forsyth County — Suwanee, Cumming, and Fowler Park areas.

Is training 1-on-1 or small group?

Both. Choose private 1-on-1 sessions for maximum individual attention, or small-group training for game-like reps with other athletes.

What should my athlete bring?

A basketball, court shoes, and water. Everything else is provided.

How do I book?

Pick a time on the booking calendar — or reach us at asia@mindfulhoops.org / (678) 341-8660.
